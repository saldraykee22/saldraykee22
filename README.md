### 🇬🇧 About me

I study Econometrics at Süleyman Demirel University, Türkiye (graduating 2027).
What I enjoy most is taking a claim, testing it against data, and reporting the result honestly, whether or not I like it.

I work with AI coding agents (Claude Code, Codex, Gemini) as a team. The agents collect data and write code.
My job is to ask the question, choose the method, and check every number against its source.
